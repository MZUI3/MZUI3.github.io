{%- comment -%} Print a post title without its leading "[...]" label, e.g. "[RL] PPO" -> "PPO". Arguments: post: the post whose title to print {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _title = include.post.title -%} {%- assign _first = _title | slice: 0 -%} {%- if _first == '[' and _title contains '] ' -%} {%- assign _label = _title | split: '] ' | first -%} {%- assign _title = _title | remove_first: _label | remove_first: '] ' -%} {%- endif -%} {{- _title -}}
