{%- comment -%} Find the series (from _data/series.yml) that a post belongs to. Arguments: post: the post to look up Sets `post_series` to the matching series, or nil when none matches. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign post_series = nil -%} {%- capture _series_pair -%}{{ include.post.categories[0] }}/{{ include.post.categories[1] }}{%- endcapture -%} {%- for _series in site.data.series -%} {%- if _series.match contains include.post.categories[0] or _series.match contains _series_pair -%} {%- assign post_series = _series -%} {%- break -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%}
